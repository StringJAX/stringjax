# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the
StringJAX community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include using
welcoming and inclusive language, being respectful of differing viewpoints, gracefully
accepting constructive criticism, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments,
personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, and publishing others'
private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported
to the maintainers at the contact address listed in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
